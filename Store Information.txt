## Cuisine

Dewdney Garden is an **Asian-fusion restaurant** serving Chinese, Vietnamese, and Thai-inspired dishes. The menu combines classic Chinese-Canadian favourites, noodle dishes, Vietnamese vermicelli bowls, Thai-style pad Thai, soups, appetizers, and group meals.

## General Restaurant Description

Dewdney Garden is a Regina Asian-fusion restaurant on Cavendish Street, serving Chinese, Vietnamese, and Thai food from one menu. Its positioning is especially strong for customers looking for fresh, made-to-order comfort food, noodles, rice dishes, appetizers, group dinners, pickup, direct delivery, and later-evening meals.

The restaurant’s own website describes its food as hot and freshly made to order. It highlights chicken balls, pad Thai, wor wonton soup, Special Cantonese Chow Mein, lemongrass vermicelli, Shanghai noodles, chicken pad Thai, spring rolls, deep-fried wontons, chop suey, and family dinners. Its direct delivery service operates seven days a week, with late-night availability on certain days.
