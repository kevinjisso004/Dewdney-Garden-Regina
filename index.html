Chinese, Vietnamese & Thai Flavours, One Regina Menu

Dewdney Garden is a Regina Asian-fusion restaurant on Cavendish Street built around a broad, made-to-order menu rather than a single cuisine. We serve Chinese-Canadian favourites, Vietnamese vermicelli bowls and Thai-style pad Thai side by side, along with soups, appetizers and generous family dinners — all cooked fresh for each order.
