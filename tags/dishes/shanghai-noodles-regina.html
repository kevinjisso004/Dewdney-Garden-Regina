About the Shanghai Noodles

Shanghai Noodles are highlighted by Dewdney Garden as one of the menu's standout noodle dishes. Thick, chewy wheat noodles are wok-tossed at high heat with fresh vegetables and your choice of protein in a savoury, lightly sweet soy-based sauce.

It's a comforting, satisfying order for lunch or dinner, and a favourite for direct delivery across Regina neighbourhoods including Glen Elm Park and Downtown. Ask about MSG-free preparation when ordering.
