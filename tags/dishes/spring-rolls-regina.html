About the Spring Rolls

Spring Rolls are a classic Dewdney Garden appetizer, hand-rolled and deep-fried to a crisp, golden finish around a seasoned vegetable filling. They're served hot and fresh, exactly the way our website describes our food: made to order.

A popular starter for family dinners, catering orders and casual takeout alike, Spring Rolls pair naturally with chicken balls, deep-fried wontons or a bowl of wor wonton soup for a well-rounded Regina takeout meal.
