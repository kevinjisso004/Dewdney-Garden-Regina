About the Chicken Balls

Chicken Balls are the dish Dewdney Garden is best known for across Regina. Tender pieces of chicken are hand-battered and deep-fried until the outside turns crispy and golden while the inside stays juicy, then served with a tangy-sweet dipping sauce made in house.

It is one of the most-ordered items on our Cavendish Street menu, and a natural pairing with fried rice or chow mein for a bigger family-style meal. Order online for pickup, direct delivery, or late-night takeout in Regina, including in Eastview and Glen Elm Park.
