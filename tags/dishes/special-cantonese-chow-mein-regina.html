About the Special Cantonese Chow Mein

Special Cantonese Chow Mein is a signature dish on Dewdney Garden's Regina menu. Egg noodles are pan-fried until crispy on the outside and tender within, then topped with a savoury combination of chicken, beef, shrimp and fresh vegetables finished in a rich brown sauce.

It's an ideal order for family dinners and group meals, with generous portions built for sharing at the table. Order it online for pickup or direct delivery anywhere in Regina, including Eastview, Glen Elm and Downtown.
