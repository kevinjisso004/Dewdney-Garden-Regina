About the Wor Wonton Soup

Wor Wonton Soup is one of the dishes Dewdney Garden is most requested for in Regina. A flavourful house-made broth is filled with delicate pork wontons alongside shrimp, chicken and crisp vegetables for a soup that's substantial enough to be a meal on its own.

It's a go-to order for cold Regina evenings, a light lunch, or as a starter alongside chow mein and fried rice for the table. Order it online for pickup or direct delivery, including late-night hours on select days.
