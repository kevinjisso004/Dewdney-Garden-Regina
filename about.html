About Dewdney Garden

Chinese, Vietnamese & Thai food, one Regina kitchen

Dewdney Garden brings Chinese, Vietnamese, and Thai flavours together in one convenient restaurant on Cavendish Street. From made-to-order pad Thai and Shanghai noodles to vermicelli bowls, chow mein, and wor wonton soup, every dish is prepared fresh for pickup, direct delivery, and family-style dining in Regina.

Our story

A Regina restaurant built around one shared menu

01

Chinese, Vietnamese & Thai food, together

Dewdney Garden has served the Cavendish Street neighbourhood in Regina for years, built around a broad, made-to-order Asian menu rather than a single cuisine. Classic Chinese-Canadian favourites sit alongside Vietnamese vermicelli bowls and Thai-style pad Thai, all cooked fresh to order.

02

A menu Regina keeps coming back to

Guest favourites include chicken balls, pad Thai, wor wonton soup, Special Cantonese chow mein, Shanghai noodles, and lemongrass vermicelli, alongside spring rolls, deep-fried wontons, and chop suey. MSG-free preparation is available on request.

03

More than a takeout counter

Beyond everyday pickup, Dewdney Garden offers family dinners for groups, catering for larger gatherings, gift certificates, and direct delivery seven days a week — with late-night availability as late as 1:30 a.m. on select evenings.
